# P2.41 – Paused Ad Continue Action

## Scope

Add a dedicated Qwen3-VL action for resuming a Fruit Blast reward/interstitial ad that is visibly paused or stopped mid-playback. Existing NEXT_AD and CLOSE behavior remains unchanged.

## Decision path

- The model may return NEXT_AD, CLOSE, CONTINUE, or WAIT.
- CONTINUE is described in the inference prompt as valid only for a visibly paused/stopped ad with an explicit resume control.
- The target must be an exact, unique, visible, enabled, clickable Accessibility element represented in the same screenshot frame.
- The frame freshness and confidence checks in AutonomousAdDecisionEngine remain mandatory.
- The service maps CONTINUE to a dedicated VisualActionType and runs it through the same authorization pipeline as all other AI actions.
- AD_PAUSED is established only when the current frame belongs to a known ad SDK Activity and the model selected CONTINUE for that frame.
- ActionExecutionPolicy permits CONTINUE only in AD_PAUSED. NEXT_AD and CLOSE remain limited to AD_PLAYING/SKIP_AVAILABLE.
- AutonomousTargetSafetyPolicy permits CONTINUE only for explicit resume labels (Continue, Resume, Play and narrowly defined variants). Promotional labels such as Play now, Install, and generic gameplay/reward targets remain denied.
- Live Accessibility matching, final foreground validation, one-attempt guard, and final click authorization remain in force.

## Fail-closed cases

- UNKNOWN, AD_COMPLETED, non-ad Activity, stale frame, low confidence, missing/duplicate target, empty label, non-clickable/disabled/invisible node, or changed foreground cannot authorize CONTINUE.
- The pixel-only VisualActionDetector does not infer CONTINUE; this action is selected by Qwen3-VL with contextual image reasoning.
- A generic "Continue" label alone does not establish a paused ad. The known ad Activity and the model's CONTINUE decision are both required.

## Tests

Added/updated unit coverage for:
- CONTINUE is rejected in UNKNOWN, AD_PLAYING, SKIP_AVAILABLE, and AD_COMPLETED.
- CONTINUE is permitted only in AD_PAUSED.
- A known ad Activity plus explicit AI continue decision is required to classify AD_PAUSED.
- Resume labels are action-specific; promotional labels remain denied.
- The decision engine preserves the model's CONTINUE choice.
